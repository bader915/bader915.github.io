About Me

I am a fresh Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate with Honours from Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU, 2026). During university and my R&D role at Control Easy, I worked across power systems, protection, PLCs, sensors, embedded hardware, industrial automation and computer vision.

At Control Easy I worked on several machine vision projects back to back, and each one taught me something the last one didn't. I started with fabric classification and object detection, where I learned the full workflow — building a dataset, training a model in Python and TensorFlow, and putting together a GUI. From there I moved to wood defect segmentation, which pushed me from basic detection into pixel-level segmentation with YOLOv8. The PCB inspection project taught me that a good model isn't the whole answer — I had to write extra logic on top of it to catch components that were missing entirely, which the model alone couldn't flag. My last project tied it together: an automated golf ball inspection station on a Raspberry Pi 5, wired to a 24V proximity sensor through a relay circuit I designed myself, running a Vision Transformer model I switched to after my first model struggled on the live camera feed. Alongside the software, I got hands-on training on Siemens S7-1200, S7-300 and S7-200 PLCs, programming in SCL through TIA Portal. Since the work was project-based, I managed my own tasks week to week, and I wrote the manuals for everything I built so the company could use it after I left.
